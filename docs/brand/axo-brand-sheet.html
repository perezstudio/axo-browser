Axo

A native, lean browser for the Apple ecosystem. Still maintained.

Marks

One symbol does everything. The solid head is the brand logo and the app icon. Its face and gill roots are carved out, so they show whatever sits behind them.

Brand logo

Blush head, Axolotl Pink gills, Raspberry face. Use it anywhere there is no Raspberry behind it: the website, social avatars, stickers, and press.

On Raspberry

The face is carved out and shows the Raspberry background, like the brand logo.

Single color

For favicons, the menu bar, and anywhere only one ink is available. It takes the text color.

App icon

The symbol centered on a Raspberry tile, face carved out to show the tile behind it. Dock, App Store, and the download page.

Axo

Lockup

Symbol to the left of the wordmark in Nunito ExtraBold. The symbol is about twice the wordmark's cap height.

Clear space

Keep at least one eye's width of empty space on every side, shown in pink. Nothing else enters that area.

Minimum size

The single-color symbol holds up down to 16 px. Below 32 px, drop the lockup and use the symbol alone.

Color

Raspberry leads, pinks carry the character, and neutrals do the everyday work. Inside the browser, pink stays an accent so it never competes with the pages people read.

Usage

Keep the symbol simple and recognizable. A few things to avoid:

Don't stretch it

Scale the symbol evenly.

Don't recolor it

Use the brand colors or a single ink.

Don't tilt it

Axo always sits level.

Don't crowd it

Place it on a calm, solid background.
